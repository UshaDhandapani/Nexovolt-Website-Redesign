SIGNATURE ENGINEERING METHODOLOGY

Find the problem. Engineer the solution. Validate the result.

Measure

Capture actual operating conditions, instrument scope and a representative baseline.

Diagnose

Identify sources, interactions and likely root causes in their system context.

Quantify

Translate the issue into measurable technical, operational or business impact.

Engineer

Select and size a suitable corrective strategy, including alternatives and limitations.

Implement

Deploy the approved scope with practical commissioning and site records.

Validate

Measure again under comparable conditions and document the achieved outcome.
