PRIVACY

Website privacy notice.

This notice describes data handled by the Nexovolt website as it is currently built.

Online enquiry form

When the Python contact service is configured and enabled, the details you submit are sent to Nexovolt's private Google Sheet and a notification is emailed to nexovoltsolutions@gmail.com so the team can respond. The form asks for your name, company, location, business email, phone, industry, requirement, site description, optional evidence description and preferred contact time. It does not accept file uploads.

The service records the total quotation as “Pending engineer review”; it does not calculate a price. Nexovolt controls access to the Sheet and Gmail account. Ask Nexovolt about access, correction or deletion of an enquiry. The data retention period must be set by Nexovolt before submissions are enabled.

Theme preference

The site stores your theme mode and, in manual mode, your chosen light or dark theme in this browser using local storage. Automatic mode follows your device local time. Theme settings are not sent to a third-party service.

Calls and email

Phone and email links open the app or service you choose. Any message you send is handled outside this website by Nexovolt and the relevant communication providers. Please do not send confidential site records unless you have agreed a secure way to share them.

Technical hosting data

This website does not include analytics, advertising scripts, or file uploads. The contact service applies basic request-rate limits and may write technical errors to the hosting logs. The hosting provider may also process connection information under its own terms; Nexovolt does not control the provider's logs.

Theme preferences remain in this browser until you clear the site data or local storage for this site in your browser settings.

For questions about a phone or email enquiry, or to ask about information you sent through this form, contact Nexovolt at nexovoltsolutions@gmail.com. Online submissions are only accepted while the contact service and privacy approval are configured.
