REUSABLE CASE-STUDY FORMAT

How Nexovolt documents a project.

This is the structure for the first approved proof library; it does not invent outcomes while approval is pending.

Business context

Why the system or process mattered, and the observed challenge or risk.

Measurement & diagnosis

Instrument, duration, location, load condition, baseline, engineering interpretation and limitations.

Selected solution

Why the corrective strategy was suitable, including relevant implementation and commissioning details.

Before & after

Comparable measurements with labelled operating conditions, axes, units and measurement points.

Measured outcome

Approved technical or business outcome only; no invented savings or universal claims.

Engineering takeaway

A transferable insight that helps a similar industrial buyer understand the issue.

VISUAL EVIDENCE STANDARDS

Data needs context to be credible.

Published visuals can include harmonic spectra, trends, voltage/current waveforms, THD/TDD or PF comparisons, and site or equipment photos where permission is in place.

  • Readable axes, units and measurement points
  • Comparable load and operating conditions
  • Clear date, duration and instrument context
  • Defined scope and technical limitations
  • Approved customer naming or anonymised descriptor
